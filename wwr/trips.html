Our Rafting Trips

River Explorer - Beginner

Perfect for beginners and families, the River Explorer trip offers a fun and relaxing introduction to whitewater rafting. Enjoy gentle rapids, beautiful natural scenery, and plenty of time to appreciate the river. Our guides will provide safety instructions and help you throughout the adventure, making this trip a great choice for anyone experiencing rafting for the first time.

Canyon Adventure - Intermediate

Get ready for more excitement on our Canyon Adventure. This trip combines moderate rapids with incredible views as you travel through beautiful river landscapes. It is ideal for people who want a more active rafting experience without facing the most difficult rapids. With the support of our experienced guides, you can enjoy an exciting combination of adventure, teamwork, and nature.

Whitewater Challenge - Advanced

For those looking for an intense adventure, the Whitewater Challenge offers powerful rapids and an exciting journey down the river. This trip is designed for adventurous participants who are comfortable with physical activity and want a greater challenge. Our guides will lead the group through demanding sections of the river while providing the necessary equipment and safety instructions for an unforgettable rafting experience.
